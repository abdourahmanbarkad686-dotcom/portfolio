Why NGOs Need Digital Systems

May 10, 2026 • Digital Transformation

Non‑profits can multiply their impact with the right tools — but most still rely on paper and spreadsheets. Here’s why going digital is no longer optional.

Read More

The problem with paper

Many NGOs in Djibouti and across the Horn of Africa still track beneficiaries, donations, and projects manually. This slows reporting, increases errors, and makes it nearly impossible to share real‑time data with donors.

What a digital system offers

A custom platform — even a simple one — can centralise all data. Donors can view live project dashboards, volunteers can log hours via a mobile interface, and field officers can update records instantly.

The real impact

When I built the Base Central CRCS dashboard for the Djibouti Red Crescent Society, reporting time dropped by 70%. That meant more time helping people and less time stuck in Excel.

Digital systems aren’t a luxury — they’re a force multiplier for organisations with a mission. If your NGO is still hesitating, now is the time to start.

Building Modern Web Apps in 2026

Apr 28, 2026 • Web Development

From React Server Components to edge databases, the way we build web apps has changed forever. Here’s what I’m excited about.

Read More

React Server Components (RSC)

RSC lets you render parts of your app on the server, sending only the HTML to the client. This means faster first loads, less JavaScript shipped, and better SEO — all without giving up interactivity.

Edge databases

Databases like PlanetScale and Turso run at the edge, close to your users. For a developer in Djibouti building apps for a global audience, this means low latency from Europe to Asia without expensive infrastructure.

All‑in‑one deployment

Platforms like Vercel, Netlify, and Railway let you push code and have it live in seconds. Combined with GitHub Actions, you can achieve fully automated CI/CD — even for a solo developer.

2026 is about building fast, resilient apps without over‑engineering. The tools are better than ever, and the barrier to entry keeps dropping.

The Importance of Responsive Design

Mar 15, 2026 • UI/UX

Over 65% of web traffic in Africa comes from mobile phones. If your website isn’t mobile‑first, you’re invisible to most of your audience.

Read More

Google’s mobile‑first indexing

Google now ranks your site based on the mobile version first. A desktop‑only layout hurts your SEO, no matter how beautiful it looks on a 27‑inch monitor.

User experience in Africa

Here in Djibouti, many users access the internet on a 4‑inch screen with intermittent connectivity. A lightweight, responsive design ensures your message reaches them — even on a 3G connection.

Practical tips

Use fluid grids, modern CSS (flexbox/grid), responsive images with srcset, and lazy loading. Test on real devices, not just Chrome DevTools. And always keep load time under 3 seconds.

Responsive design isn’t a trend — it’s the foundation of a professional web presence today. I treat it as a non‑negotiable in every project I deliver.
