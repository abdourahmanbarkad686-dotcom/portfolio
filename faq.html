Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before we start

How long does a typical project take?

Most projects take between 1 to 4 weeks, depending on complexity. A simple portfolio or landing page can be ready in under a week. Larger systems like dashboards or e‑commerce platforms may take 3–4 weeks. I always provide a clear timeline before we begin.

Do you work with clients outside Djibouti?

Yes, absolutely. I work remotely with international clients across Africa, Europe, and the Middle East. All communication can be done via email, WhatsApp, or video calls — distance is never a problem.

What technologies do you use?

My core stack includes React, Node.js, MongoDB, ASP.NET, and Python. For styling I use modern CSS, Tailwind, and Bootstrap. I choose the best technology based on your project's specific needs.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on the scope. A portfolio site starts at $80, a business website at $150, and custom dashboard systems at $400+. Check the Pricing page for full details. I also offer custom quotes for unique projects.

What does the process look like?

1. Discovery – We discuss your goals.
2. Proposal – I send a detailed plan.
3. Design – You review mockups.
4. Development – I build and share updates.
5. Launch – Final testing and handover.

Do you offer maintenance after the project?

Yes, I offer ongoing maintenance packages for security updates, content changes, and performance monitoring. We can arrange a monthly retainer or hourly support.

What if I don't like the initial design?

Every project includes revision rounds (usually 2–4 depending on the plan). I listen to your feedback and refine until you're completely satisfied.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes, I typically ask for a 30–50% deposit to begin work, with the remaining balance due upon project completion. This protects both of us.

Can you build mobile apps?

I specialize in responsive web applications that work perfectly on mobile browsers. For native mobile apps, I can collaborate with mobile developers or recommend trusted partners.

How do I get started?

Simply send me a message or reach out on WhatsApp at +253 77 05 77 16. Tell me about your project, and I'll reply within 24 hours.

Still have questions?

I'm happy to answer them personally.
